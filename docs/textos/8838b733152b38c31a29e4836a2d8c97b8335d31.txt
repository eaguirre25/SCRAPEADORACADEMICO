                                         Abstract
         The objectives of this research were to: 1) study the framework of strengthening
the consciousness and practices of students’ awareness about global warming; and
2) study how school management strengthens the consciousness and practices for students
as well as analyzing weaknesses, opportunities and treats of the management committee.
This mixed method research collected data from 395 primary schools under the office of
the Basic Education Commission. The instruments used in this study were questionnaires
and the strategic evaluation form to testify feasibility and appropriateness.
         The results showed that: 1) the framework consists of two school management
committees: academic affair and general management committees, and the committee
to strengthen the consciousness and practices of students; and 2) overall, the current
state of management was considered to be in the middle level (M = 3.349). Considering
each aspect, facility management and learning center had the highest average
(M = 3.553). The desirable state of management was performed at a high level (M = 3.839
as a whole). While considering each aspect, facility management and learning center had
the high average (M = 3.946); 3) The strengths of management were learning and facility
environments as well as school learning center. However, the weaknesses of management
were curriculum development, measurement, and evaluation along with the student affairs.
The opportunities for management committee were government policy and society. While
the threats for management were the economy and technology.

Keywords: school management, strengthen the consciousness and practices, global warming
