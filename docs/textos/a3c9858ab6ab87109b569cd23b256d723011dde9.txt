                                             This review article is the result of research on public policy, educational management
                                             and teacher training in Colombia. For its development, some concepts in which
                                             education policy is framed and the relevance that it has in the educational processes
                                             carried out in the country are described. Likewise, the concepts that have been
                                             established from different perspectives for public policy, educational management,
                                             teacher training, the relationship that these concepts have with the being and the
                                             duty to be, and the challenges of teacher training in relation to the approaches of
                                             educational policies are presented. From this, it can be concluded that education
                                             policies must be created on the principle of transversality, from which they take each
                                             of the important elements to provide a quality education that directly contributes to
                                             the development and resolution of social problems.
