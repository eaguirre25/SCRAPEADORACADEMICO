   Development of the Teamwork Inventory for Community-Based
        School Management: Viewpoint of Students’ Parents
            TAKUYA YOSHIDA (GIFU SHOTOKU GAKUEN UNIVERSITY), HIROYUKI YOSHIZAWA (GIFU UNIVERSITY),
           RYOSUKE ASANO (KURUME UNIVERSITY), RYUICHI TAMAI (KOCHI UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY) AND
                              TOSHIKAZU YOSHIDA (GIFU SHOTOKU GAKUEN UNIVERSITY)
                         JAPANESE JOURNAL OF EDUCATIONAL PSYCHOLOGY, 2020, 68, 147−159


  The aim of the present study was to develop the Teamwork Inventory for Community-based School Management
(TICSM) and to examine its reliability and validity. The participants in interviews conducted for item collection
were individuals who were concerned about elementary and middle schools, including parents, community residents,
and teachers. Based on an analysis of the inter views, items were constructed for the Teamwork Inventor y for
Community-based School Management for parents. The parents of elementary and middle school students (N＝
495) completed the inventory in a survey conducted on line. Exploratory and confirmatory factor analyses revealed
that the scale was comprised of 3 factors: team orientation, team leadership, and team process. Correlations of
each factor of the scale with trust in teachers and community residents, and group identity indicated the construct
validity of the inventory. How to utilize the scale in education settings was discussed.
     Key Words: teamwork, community-based school, parents, community residents, Teamwork Inventor y for
Community-based School Management
