Introduction
Student performance is greatly influenced by secondary school management, especially in
developing provinces like Pakistan's Punjab, where educational options and resources are

School administrators must be bold and creative (Jamil, 2024).
They are crucial to hiring new teachers, providing ongoing training, and evaluating their work, all
of which have an immediate effect on student engagement and classroom quality. In order to
support struggling students and lower dropout rates while enhancing overall academic
achievement, administrators also keep an eye on student attendance, deal with behavioural
concerns, and put remedial programs into place (Malik et al., 2025; Nicholson-Booker, 2024).
By working with parents, neighbourhood associations, and governmental entities to obtain more
funding and support for the school, an efficient secondary school administration encourages
community involvement. Students benefit from a more extensive support system and improved
learning quality because of this community partnership, which also helps close educational gaps
(Javed et al., 2020; Nwuke & Nwanguma, 2024).
In addition to task management, administration is essentially people-centered, emphasizing
collaboration, establishing a single goal, and inspiring team members to achieve it. Given its
emphasis on efficiency and coordination, administration is crucial in all fields since it enables
businesses to run effectively, adapt to change, and continuously seek expansion and progress
(Lenee et al., 2024).
Improving the quality of education in schools is largely dependent on effective management
(Abbas et al., 2021). Effective teaching and learning can thrive in an atmosphere that is created by
a proactive and well-organised administrative approach. To ensure that the school fulfils high
academic and operational standards, effective resource management, staff motivation, strategic
planning, and ongoing evaluation are all essential components of strong school administration
(Liu, 2024).
Punjab school administrators deal with particular difficulties, including a lack of funding, poor
facilities, and a scarcity of instructional materials. If these issues are not resolved, students'
learning experiences and results may suffer. Administrators who place a high value on community
involvement and form alliances with neighbourhood groups, however, frequently come up with
innovative solutions to these issues. Interacting with community stakeholders, for instance, may
result in volunteer programs or gifts that enhance the educational setting (Strait & Lima, 2023).
For instance, in educational administration, administrators supervise teacher performance,
establish a learning environment that fosters student growth, and guarantee adherence to norms
and policies. Similar to this, corporate administration duties may involve managing budgets,
organizing groups, and making the most use of available resources in order to boost output and
preserve operational effectiveness (Jerab, 2023).
Improving the standard of education in schools is largely dependent on effective management. An
environment that is conducive to effective teaching and learning is created by a proactive and well-
organized administrative approach (Sajid et al., 2022). To guarantee that the school meets high
operational and academic standards, effective resource management, staff motivation, strategic
planning, and ongoing evaluation are all essential components of strong school administration
(Arobiole & Chinyere, 2023).

The management of secondary school has a direct impact on the development of students;
therefore, it is very important to provide a conducive environment for learning. It is the
responsibility of the principal and head teacher at secondary schools to create a safe, engaging and
structured learning environment where students can thrive. They set the standards for behaviour
and intellectual achievement. Have a clear vision and communicate effectively. Additionally, make
sure that daily activities and school goals are the same (Yahya & Nur, 2023).
Effective management entails the efficient use of resources, including personnel, financing, and
educational materials. To guarantee that educators and learners have access to the materials
required for instruction. By providing teachers with chances for professional development,
administrators raise the standard of education. impact pupils' learning outcomes (Okonkwo, 2024).
Implementing the rules pertaining to education, discipline, attendance, and assessment is another
important function of the administrators. They offer the structure required for dependable and
efficient instruction. Additionally, by encouraging inclusivity, respect, and emotional support, they
create a secure and encouraging learning atmosphere (Kumar & Limbachiya, 2023).
A positive school culture is influenced by all of these. Outside of the classroom, administrators
work to establish connections with parents and the community because they are aware that parental
participation and teamwork boost students' motivation and academic achievement (Kumar &
Limbachiya, 2023).
The foundation of a good educational institution is its secondary school administration, due to the
fact that they continuously assess, monitor, and alter policies and procedures in response to student
needs and performance data. It aids educators and learners in achieving their learning objectives.
The administration is mostly responsible for providing the framework, direction, and assistance
required for efficient teaching and learning in the educational system (Kumar & Limbachiya,

Administrators at all levels are responsible for establishing educational standards, enforcing rules,

Legislators, district managers, and school principals are among them. Effective school operations

Research Methodology
This study was descriptive in nature, and a quantitative research design was used to evaluate the
objective of the study. The total population of this study was all the administrators of the school
education department in Punjab. A total of (4300) administrators, including (Head Teachers,
Principal, DEO Secondary and CEO,s) of secondary schools in Punjab province, was the
population of this study. The sample size was 400 administrators from the school education
department in the southern Punjab. In the first step, the researchers used convenient sample
technique in selecting the district and schools for data collection. In the second step, the researcher
used a simple random sampling technique in the selection of administrators from different schools
                                                3120

            The Critical Review of Social Sciences Studies, Volume 3, Number 4, 2025


by giving an equal chance to the respondents being selected. The self-developed questionnaire was
used for the data collection from respondents. The questionnaire was based on three parts. The first
part of the questionnaire depended on demographic information, e.g., Name, Institution, Gender,
Age, Designation, Scale, and Sector. The second part of the questionnaire was based on closed-
ended statements. The researchers constructed the questionnaire with 28 items, which was further
divided into 3 factors. The five-point Likert scale was used: strongly agree, agree, undecided,
disagree and strongly disagree. The questionnaire was discussed with School heads and groups of
M.Phil Scholars for content, face and construct validity of the research instrument. They suggested
some improvements in grammar and spelling. Their suggestions were incorporated accordingly
before the collection of the data. The reliability of the questionnaire was assured through pilot
testing. For this purpose, 30 administrators were selected from District Rahim Yar Khan, and a
questionnaire was developed questionnaire was divided among administrators, and the filled
questionnaire's Cronbach' alpha was used to measure the stability of the research tool and the value
of Cronbach' Alpha (0.82), which shows that the tool was highly reliable. The researchers selected
southern Punjab for the data collection. After selecting the 36 districts of Punjab, twelve schools
from each district were selected, including six male and six female schools and DEO,s Secondary
and CEO,s by using a simple random sampling technique. Prior permission from the school
education department was taken for the collection of data. After getting the permission for data
collection, the researcher attended the selected schools and delivered the questionnaire to the
administrators and gave them proper guidelines in filling the questionnaire. Data were analyzed
using SPSS 22 software with descriptive statistics including standard deviation, mean and
percentage.

Conclusion
The findings demonstrate that secondary school teachers are actively involved in creating a
positive learning environment and are viewed by both students and teachers as being essential to
students' academic achievement. Teachers participate in administration-facilitated decision-making
processes, feel a sense of ownership and responsibility for their responsibilities, and make a
substantial contribution to student performance. A supportive environment is created by this
cooperative approach, which guarantees that teachers' opinions are respected and that they are
committed to the growth of their students and the advancement of the school.
According to the findings, secondary school teachers are praised for encouraging a polite,
cooperative learning atmosphere among their students, which upholds their dignity and teamwork.
Teachers' use of varied instruction and personalised learning has a favourable influence on
students' academic success and growth, demonstrating how well-suited these teaching strategies
are to a range of student demands. When combined, these strategies foster a welcoming and
inclusive learning environment that raises student achievement and engagement.
Students benefit greatly when a principal prioritises student wellbeing and is dedicated to making a
positive school culture. The importance of a well-equipped and encouraging environment for
student performance is further highlighted by the perception that efficient resource allocation by
school administration is necessary to promote and improve learning outcomes.

Recommendations
The following recommendations were made according to the findings of this study:
      Hold frequent training sessions to improve school administrators' managerial and
       leadership abilities. This will assist them in improving the learning environment.
      Make sure secondary schools have enough resources to support students' learning and
       wellbeing, including classrooms, libraries, and clean water.
      Give schools enough resources, such as textbooks, instructional materials, and technology,
       so that administrators can assist instructors and students in an efficient manner.
      Involve parents and the neighborhood in school events to build a network of support for
       children's academic achievement.
      Create a system for routinely assessing school administrators' performance and how they
       affect students' results in order to pinpoint and correct any shortcomings.
      Policies that directly benefit students, such as enhancing instruction, lowering absenteeism,
       and encouraging extracurricular activities, should be given first priority by administrators.
