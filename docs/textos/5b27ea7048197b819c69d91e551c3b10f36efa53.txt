Abstract: Curriculum renewal influences not only
educational content, but also overall school management
practices. In this study, we investigate the impact of
curriculum reform on school management practices with an
interdisciplinary approach. Through a literature review, we
identified various forms of impact, including changes in
management paradigm, organizational structure, school
culture, teacher skills, education quality, community
involvement, management skills, resource quality, student
involvement, and service quality. Our findings suggest that
school management must adapt planning, training, resource
provision, and community involvement to manage change.

Introduction
       In the era of globalization and rapidly developing technology, the
educational curriculum must continue to be updated to suit the demands
of the times and students' needs.1 However, curriculum reform does not
only involve changes in educational content, but also influences the way
schools are managed and organized.2 This phenomenon covers various
aspects: (1) Increased acceleration of change, (2) Diversification of the
curriculum, (3) Changes in the role of school principals and, (4) Increased
Stakeholder Collaboration. This phenomenon shows that curriculum
updates not only affect learning content, but also change the overall
school management landscape3 Therefore, it is important for school
management to understand the impact of curriculum reforms and
develop appropriate strategies to manage changes effectively to improve
the quality of education in their schools.
       Curriculum renewal is an important effort to respond to the
demands of current developments and prepare students to face future
challenges.4 This can be seen in various countries, including Indonesia,
curriculum renewal is often considered a crucial step in improving the
quality of education.5 However, changes in the curriculum do not only
cover aspects of subject matter, but also influence various aspects of
