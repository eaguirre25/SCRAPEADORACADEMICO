Influencing Factors and Intervention Strategies of
Teachers' Work Engagement in Primary and Secondary
Schools
Hongxiu Tan*
Teacher Education College, Shaoguan University, Shaoguan, Guangdong, China


Abstract: In recent years, the job burnout rate of teachers in my country has been on the rise. With the rise of positive
psychology, researchers began to study teachers' work engagement. After consulting the literature, combined with the actual
situation of primary and secondary school teachers, it is found that the main factors affecting the work engagement of primary
and secondary school teachers are: teachers' salary, teachers' professional identity, teachers' professional development platform,
and school management system. Therefore, it is possible to improve the work commitment of primary and secondary school
teachers from the aspects of improving the existing system, improving the level of school management, and teachers' own efforts.
Keywords: Primary and secondary school teachers, Work engagement, Influencing factors, Intervention strategies.
