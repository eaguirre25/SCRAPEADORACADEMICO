Introduction
Effective school management refers to the ability of school leaders and administrators to organize, plan, and control resources,
people, and processes to achieve educational goals and improve student outcomes (Bush & Glover, 2016) [4]. It involves
leadership that fosters a positive school climate, effective teaching and learning practices, proper resource allocation,
accountability, and strong community engagement. According to Leithwood & Louis (2021) [22] noted that of all the factors
that contribute to what students learn at school, leadership is second in strength only to classroom instruction. Globally,
research highlights that several interrelated factors determine effective school management, many of which are highly relevant
to public secondary schools in Lusaka District. A systematic review concluded that strong leadership, effective teaching
practices, a positive school culture and parental involvement” are core determinants of school effectiveness (Harris &
Chapman, 2020) [15]. Similarly, Karadağ et al. (2019) [20] emphasize that a school should have a culture that values the
professional development of its teachers, collegiality, collaborative leadership, and teamwork to be effective. Martinez &
Rodriguez (2021) [25] further assert that of all the factors that contribute to what students learn at school, leadership is second in
strength only to classroom instruction. These studies collectively demonstrate that effective leadership, positive school culture,
strong community involvement, and adequate resources are globally recognized as the foundation for improving school
outcomes.
Effective school management plays a critical role in ensuring the delivery of quality education and improved student outcomes.
