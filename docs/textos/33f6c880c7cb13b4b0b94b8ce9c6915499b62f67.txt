Submission March 2023
Revised March 2023                     This study explored the perception of public-school teachers in Zone 1,
Accepted March 2023                    Division of Zambales towards the management practices of the school
                                       heads during the COVID-19 Pandemic. Data on teachers’ work perfor-
*Corresponding author:                 mance and the academic performance of the pupils were also consid-
E-mail:                                ered. The researcher-made questionnaire was used as the data collec-
Efigenia.fontillas@deped.gov.ph        tion tool in a descriptive-survey research design with 352 public ele-
                                       mentary school teachers. The school management practices included
                                       intra-school management and inter-school management practices. The
                                       study showed that the teacher respondents are rated outstanding in
                                       their work performance amidst COVID-19 and pupils attained a “Profi-
                                       cient” level of their academic performance as well. The study also re-
                                       vealed that there is no significant relationship between teachers' work
                                       performance and intra-school management practices, whereas there is
                                       a significant relationship between teachers' work performance and in-
                                       ter-school management practices. Furthermore, the study determined
                                       that there was a substantial correlation between the academic achieve-
                                       ment of the students and the intra-school and inter-school management
                                       practices.
