         Abstract
         The study determines the relationship between the utilization of the Maintenance and Other Operating Expenses
         (MOOE) and key school attributes, including teachers' welfare, development, and school operations. This study was
         conducted in Tagoloan District, Division of Lanao del Norte, during the School Year 2024-2025. Employing
         Descriptive Correlational analysis, this study examines the impact of awareness of policy documents and active
         teacher management on teachers' welfare, and the influence of these factors on students' welfare and school operations.
         Findings revealed a significant relationship between active teacher management and both teachers' and students'
         welfare. Conversely, awareness of policy documents showed no significant relationship with students' welfare. It
         indicates that active teacher management is the strongest predictor of effective MOOE utilization. Regression analysis
         was conducted to determine the predictive power of various indicators, including demographic and professional
         factors. For teachers' welfare and development, active teacher management emerged as the most significant predictor
         explaining a substantial proportion of variance. Other factors, such as age, sex, civil status, highest educational
         attainment, Plantilla position, and awareness of policy documents, were not statistically significant. These findings
         highlight the importance of active teacher management in maximizing the use of MOOE to promote both teacher and
         student development. Recommendations include strengthening teacher engagement and management practices to
         maximize the efficiency and effectiveness of budget allocation.
         Keywords: resource allocation, school budgeting, operational expenses, financial of school operational expenses

Introduction
This dynamic interplay between educational management practices and financial resource allocation is critical in fostering an
environment conducive to academic excellence and holistic development. First, understanding the expenses in MOOE helps to manage
the budgets effectively, identifying areas where costs can be reduced without compromising quality. Second, knowledge of maintenance
processes can improve operational efficiency, ensuring equipment and facilities run smoothly and leading to increased productivity.
This ensures that funds are efficiently allocated to support essential company operations. In the contemporary educational landscape,
ensuring the delivery of high-quality education has become paramount for institutions striving to meet global standards and national
benchmarks.
Quality education management encompasses a broad spectrum of practices, including the development and enforcement of policy
documents, as well as the active management of teaching staff. Awareness and adherence to educational policies provide a framework
for institutional governance, guiding decision-making processes and ensuring consistency in educational delivery. Concurrently, active
teacher management—encompassing recruitment, professional development, and performance evaluation—directly influences the
quality of instruction and overall school effectiveness. These elements collectively form the bedrock upon which the quality of
education is built and maintained.
The utilization of MOOE is a crucial aspect of school management, directly influencing the operational and developmental capabilities
of educational institutions. MOOE funds are allocated for various purposes, including school operations and development, teacher
welfare and professional development, and student welfare and support services. Efficient and strategic utilization of these funds
ensures that schools can maintain infrastructure, provide necessary resources, and support both teachers and students in achieving
educational objectives. However, the relationship between quality education management and MOOE utilization is not straightforward
and may be significantly influenced by the demographic profile of those managing and utilizing these resources. Factors such as age,
sex, civil status, highest educational attainment, and Plantilla position of the respondents may affect their perspectives on policy
implementation and resource management. Understanding these demographic influences is crucial for developing targeted strategies
that address the distinct needs and contexts of various educational settings.
This study smart the impact of quality education management on the utilization of maintenance and other operating expenses, with a
particular focus on how awareness of policy documents and active teacher management practices contribute to financial decision-
making processes in schools. By examining the interplay between these variables, the study aims to provide insights into optimizing
resource allocation to enhance school operations, teacher development, and student outcomes within the Tagoloan District.
Furthermore, this study also explored the challenges faced in the effective utilization of MOOE funds, aiming to identify potential
bottlenecks and propose strategic solutions through empirical analysis and insights drawn from the experiences of educators in
Tagoloan District, Division of Lanao del Norte for the School year 2024-2025.

Maintenance and Other Operating Expenses (MOOE) in schools will be provided in 2024. This strategy places a strong emphasis on
ensuring that money reaches the schools directly, promoting effective and transparent use. It mandates that schools use MOOE for
items such as training, instructional materials, and minor repairs that are directly related to learning goals. In this connection, the
directive of DepEd Order 007, s. In 2024, a measure is introduced to improve fund management at the school level. For example,

The contents of this IRR are consistent with the GOP's commitment to promoting good governance and its efforts to apply the values
of openness, accountability, fairness, efficiency, and economy in its procurement process. The GOP believes that procurement of
infrastructure projects, products, and consulting services should be open and conducted through public bidding unless specifically
specified in this IRR under the Alternative modalities of procurement. (RIRR for Republic Act No. 9184).

Methodology
Research Design
The study employed a descriptive-correlational research method to assess the quality of educational management and the utilization of
school maintenance and other operational costs in Tagoloan District, Lanao del Norte, during the 2024-2025 school year. To provide
a comprehensive understanding of the variables under examination, this technique combines descriptive and correlational methods.
In this study, descriptive research is ideal for providing a thorough depiction of the existing state or condition of a specific phenomenon
or problem, as opposed to a correlational approach. On the other hand, it was used to identify and examine any existing correlations
between several variables. Specifically, the study sought to establish a link between management quality education, school MOOE
support for the school implementation program, and teacher performance.
Respondents
The respondents in this study were teachers from the Tagoloan district in Lanao del Norte. This district represents a diverse educational
landscape, encompassing both urban and rural schools, which provides a rich context for assessing the quality of educational
management and the use of Maintenance and Other Operating Expenses (MOOE) funds. These teachers are important stakeholders in
the educational system, directly influencing the quality of instruction and the effective implementation of educational policies. Their
insights and experiences are invaluable for understanding how management practices impact educational outcomes and how MOOE
funds are allocated and utilized within their schools.
By including teachers from various schools in the Tagoloan district, the study ensured a comprehensive analysis of educational
management and resource utilization. These respondents, who interact daily with students and are involved in the practical aspects of
educational delivery, offer a ground-level perspective on the challenges and successes related to MOOE usage. Their feedback offers
a nuanced understanding of how management practices influence classroom dynamics, resource allocation, and overall educational
quality. This approach enables a comprehensive evaluation of the district's current educational management state and identifies areas
for improvement to enhance the effectiveness of MOOE expenditures in supporting educational objectives.
The schools in Tagoloan District in this study cover Cabasaran Elementary School, Darimbang Elementary School, Dimayon
Elementary School, Gadongan Elementary School, Kiasar Integrated School, Lininding Integrated School, Malimbato Primary School,
Payawan Elementary School, Sultan Palao Ali Memorial Elementary School, Tagoloan Integrated School, Tagoloan National High
School, and Tumple Integrated School. Division of Lanao del Norte, during the School Year 2024-2025. This study employed a target
population to provide a comprehensive view of educational management quality, school operating expenditures, and school
implementation programs, as well as teacher performance.
Instrument
The study employed standard questionnaires to investigate the relationship between educational management and school MOOE
support for the school implementation program, as well as teachers' teaching performance. These surveys serve as the foundation for
data collection. Part 1 of the questionnaire included the instructors' profiles, detailing their age, gender, marital status, educational
background, and years of service. Part 2 focused on gathering information from teachers about the impact of school MOOE assistance
on the implementation program in terms of teacher resource allocation and usage, infrastructure and facility upgrades, and professional
development opportunities. The instructors were asked to score their agreement or disagreement with these assertions on a 4-point scale
from Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree.
In a study by Israel (2023) on the Effects of the Attributes of School Management Teams on the Management of Quality Education
Towards School Effectiveness, various attributes of school management teams and their influence on educational quality were explored.
The instrument is structured into several sections, each targeting different dimensions of school management that contribute to effective
educational outcomes. The questionnaire incorporates a Likert-scale tool to capture quantitative data.
The questionnaire by Israel (2023) is a meticulously crafted tool aimed at dissecting the complexities of school management and its
direct correlation with the quality of education provided. This questionnaire is divided into two parts. The first section focuses on
raising the understanding of policy texts that inform education quality management. It has seven items with a Likert scale of 1) strongly
disagree, 2) disagree, 3) agree, and 4) strongly agree. The second part of the questionnaire addressed active teacher management,
comprising 14 items with the same Likert scale as the first part.
In addition, the second questionnaire in the study by Arevalo and Comighud (2020), on the "Utilization of Maintenance and Other
Operating Expenses (MOOE) concerning Students' Academic Performance," examined how the allocation and usage of MOOE funds
impact academic outcomes. The questionnaire is designed to collect data on various aspects of MOOE utilization. Another part of the
questionnaire assesses the perceived impact of MOOE expenditures on students' academic performance. Respondents provide their
views on how well-managed MOOE funds contribute to enhancing learning environments, improving teaching resources, and
ultimately influencing student outcomes. This section may include questions that link specific MOOE expenditures to changes in
academic metrics such as test scores, attendance rates, and student engagement.

Procedure
For the study on the management quality of education and the utilization of Maintenance and Other Operating Expenses (MOOE), the
data collection process involves systematically gathering information from teachers in five schools within the Tagoloan district of
Lanao del Norte. The study included the teaching staff from each school to ensure comprehensive data collection and to accurately
reflect the perspectives on MOOE utilization and educational management quality. To obtain necessary permissions from the
Department of Education (DepEd) superintendent in Lanao del Norte and to arrange schedules with the principals of the respective
schools. The researcher ensured compliance with ethical standards, including informed consent from participants. The researcher
coordinated with school principals to schedule data collection sessions, ensuring minimal disruption to school activities.
The researcher personally conducted the study with the respondents. She distributed the printed questionnaires to all teachers in the
selected schools: Cabasaran Elementary School (9 teachers) Darimbang Elementary School(5 teachers) Dimayon Elementary School
(6 teachers) Gadongan Elementary School (8 teachers), Kiasar Integrated School (10 teachers) Lininding Integrated School, (10
teachers) Malimbato Primary School (4 teachers) Payawan Elementary School (9 teachers) Sultan Palao Ali Memorial Elementary
School (5 teachers) Tagoloan Integrated School (11 teachers) Tagoloan National High School (12 teachers) and Tumple Integrated
School (11 teachers). Furthermore, the researcher provided clear instructions for completing the questionnaire, enabling respondents
to answer it thoroughly.
Data Analysis
These statistical methods can give insights and interpretations of the study's data, as well as help answer research questions and
objectives.
Frequency and Percentage Distribution. This was used to describe the respondents' characteristics and their teaching performance in
terms of Satisfaction.
Mean and Standard Deviation. This was used to quantify and describe teachers' perceptions of the influence of school MOOE assistance
on school implementation programs in terms of instructional resource allocation and usage, infrastructure and facility enhancement,
and professional development opportunities.
Pearson's r Correlation Coefficient. This was used to determine whether there is a relationship between teacher satisfaction and their
evaluation of the impact of school MOOE assistance on the school implementation program.
Linear Regression. Regression analysis was used to identify which factors had a significant impact on respondents' opinions. The
researcher used multiple regression analysis to analyze the influence of many independent factors (allocation and use of teaching
resources, infrastructure and facility upgrades, and professional development opportunities) on a single dependent variable (teacher
performance).
The analysis indicated the extent to which each independent variable contributed to the variation in the dependent variable and identified
the major predictors of teachers' attitudes toward MOOE.

Conclusions
The study's findings led to the following conclusions. This study concluded that teachers widely acknowledge the positive impact of
School Maintenance and Other Operating Expenses (MOOE) support on school implementation programs. Across key components,
including the allocation and utilization of teaching resources, infrastructure and facility enhancements, and professional development
opportunities, educators expressed unanimous agreement regarding the benefits of MOOE assistance. Furthermore, the study revealed
a high level of teacher performance, with the majority rated as "Very Satisfactory." These results underscore the importance of
prioritizing investments in MOOE support and teacher development programs to uphold and enhance the quality of education delivery.
This study found that demographic and professional characteristics impact teachers' attitudes toward School Maintenance and Other
Operating Expenses (MOOE) assistance. Notably, those aged 36-40 and those with 21-25 years of service had different perspectives
on MOOE usage. While certain criteria, such as education level and teaching designation, influence teacher effectiveness, others, such
as age, gender, civil status, and years of service, have no significant impact. Furthermore, there is no significant association between
teacher performance and perceptions of MOOE assistance across school implementation domains. These findings underscore the
