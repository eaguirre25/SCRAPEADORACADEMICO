                                                                  ABSTRACT
                                                                  The research "Impact of Teleworking on Educational Management of the Faculty of
                                                                  Business Sciences - National University of Education Enrique Guzmán y Valle, aims to
                                                                  determine the impact of teleworking on educational management of the Faculty of
                                                                  Business Sciences of the National University of Education Enrique Guzmán y Valle;
                                                                  identifying and evaluating the behavior, characteristics and cost-benefit of the educational
                                                                  service. The techniques and instruments for data collection were the bibliographic
                                                                  compilation, the observation and the interview to a total of 04 teachers and 02 directors.
                                                                  The qualitative approach of the work has made it possible to describe the observable
                                                                  attitudes of the teachers of the analysis unit, in the context of Teleworking implemented
                                                                  by the COVID 19 health emergency. The content of the interviews systematized in the
                                                                  categorization and tax relief matrices and analyzed with the Atlas ti software, allowed to
                                                                  obtain consistent analyzes regarding teleworking and its impact on the educational
                                                                  management of the Faculty of Business Sciences of the UNE.
