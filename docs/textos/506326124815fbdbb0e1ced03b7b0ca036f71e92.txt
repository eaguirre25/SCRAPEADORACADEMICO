Abstract: Information and communication technology, represented by computer and network technology, is greatly changing
the way of life and communication of human beings. Therefore, in the 21st century, traditional schools and traditional education
systems around the world will be presented with both opportunities and challenges for new development prospects. The
construction of educational leadership will be an important key to improve the quality and charm of teachers. The United Nations
Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) brings together transformative visions, transformative knowledge
systems and transformative skills, It is regarded as the core point in the goal framework of educational leadership construction.
The improvement of educational "leadership" is mainly carried out by improving the ability to construct multi-dimensional
"change vision", expanding change knowledge based on diversified knowledge system, and improving "change skills" based on
optimization management. The above concepts and practices reflect the characteristics of emphasizing the organic combination
of international and local strategic thinking, the organic combination of knowledge mastery and innovation, and the organic
combination of professional and scientific teaching management, which have an important impact on the development trend of
China's educational leadership.
Keywords: Globalization; Educational; Ieadership; Vision for change; Direction.
