                                               On the one hand, advances in scientific research have broadened the horizons of
                                           educational leadership, moving beyond approaches focused solely on school
                                           management and incorporating new ethical, relational, and distributed perspectives.
                                           Simultaneously, knowledge in ESD has revealed that the success of initiatives in this
                                           area depends as much on pedagogical practices as on the organizational and cultural
                                           conditions of the institutions that make them possible. From this perspective,
                                           leadership and ESD are two intimately related spheres, whose joint study is
                                           fundamental and essential for addressing present and future challenges.
                                               Reflecting this, this special issue brings together a series of research projects,
                                           developed in different contexts and educational levels, that explore the potential of
                                           leadership to foster more sustainable educational organizations and practices. The
                                           contributions, grounded in a wide variety of theoretical and methodological
                                           approaches, offer a broad overview of the personal, organizational, and contextual
                                           factors that shape the transformation processes within educational spaces.
                                               The articles are organized into four main thematic sections. The first examines the
                                           impact of leadership styles in educational institutions and the challenges faced by
                                           school leaders at different levels of the system. The second analyzes the importance of
                                           institutional cultures, context, and collaboration among educational stakeholders for
                                           the implementation of ESD. The third section focuses on teacher training, highlighting
                                           the need to strengthen professional skills, teacher leadership, and institutional support
                                           for integrating ESD into classrooms. Finally, the fourth section compiles a series of
